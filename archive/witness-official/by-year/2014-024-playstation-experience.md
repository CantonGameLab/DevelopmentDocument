Last weekend, Jon, Andy and Luis headed to Vegas to show the game to the public for the first time!

We spent over a week play testing,  bug fixing, designing the booth, printing banners, and re-designing the website. It was encouraging to see that the game was already in very good shape and nothing major was broken...getting it ready for the event almost took less effort than getting decent prints of the banners from a print shop.

Here are some images of the booth:

[![2014-12-09](http://the-witness.net/news/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/2014-12-09-512x150.jpg)](http://the-witness.net/news/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/2014-12-09.jpg)[![2014-12-05](http://the-witness.net/news/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/2014-12-05-300x225.jpg)](http://the-witness.net/news/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/2014-12-05.jpg) [![bookmarks](http://the-witness.net/news/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/bookmarks-300x223.jpg)](http://the-witness.net/news/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/bookmarks.jpg)

People loved the game! Even those who came in with doubts ended up playing for long periods of time, and we got all very positive comments. The line in front of the booth was huge, it would have been great to have a bigger area with more PS4s.

[![2014-12-09 (1)](http://the-witness.net/news/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/2014-12-09-1-300x225.jpg)](http://the-witness.net/news/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/2014-12-09-1.jpg)[![line](http://the-witness.net/news/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/line-300x223.jpg)](http://the-witness.net/news/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/line.jpg)
