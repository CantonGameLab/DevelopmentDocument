  17. Matthew VanDevander
    June 20, 2014 at 2:00 pm

    The game actually does not have any ambient background music, instead focusing on naturalistic environmental sound. So you can expect to only hear the sounds that you would actually hear if you were in the type of environment that you are in. In other words, you will only hear grass rustling if there is grass rustling nearby. Perhaps there may be music in the game, but it would have to be coming out of some speaker in the environment. And it would have to have a good reason to be there beyond just setting a mood.

    For a little bit more on the sound design, you can read these two posts from the Wabi Sabi Sound blog, as they are the studio being contracted to do sound work on the game:

    http://wabisabisound.com/blog/the-witness-audio-1000-subtle-layers/

    http://wabisabisound.com/blog/witness-why-it-doesnt-need-a-mixer/

    And as a random trivia, the game has over 1,000 footstep sounds.
